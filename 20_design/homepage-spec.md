# Homepage Build Spec

## Dominant job
Convert a worksite/fleet/crew coordinator into a saved planning request.

## Two-second message
- What: 100% plant-based culture kitchen.
- Where: Puerto Vallarta.
- For whom: worksites first, with pop-ups and events.
- Next action: Book a worksite lunch.

## Golden conversion slice
1. Hero — category, place, worksite promise, primary CTA.
2. What it is — one operating rule and one business model sentence.
3. Worksite process — site → plan → onsite handoff.
4. Sample menu direction — honest, unpriced, not presented as daily availability.
5. Dome vision — conceptual status labeled.
6. Nanny story — only verified family facts.
7. Pop-ups/events — secondary lane.
8. Worksite lead form — real persistence and honest success/error states.

## Acceptance criteria
- `/` no longer shows upstream Chef by Convex marketing.
- Existing `/nanny` AI OS remains reachable.
- Primary CTA appears in header and hero and goes to the same worksite flow.
- No invented address, hours, partners, price, availability, or delivery integration.
- Lead form requires contact name, location, and email or phone.
- Successful submission is persisted through Convex before success is shown.
- Failure does not show false success.
- 320/360/375/390/414/430 widths have no unintended horizontal overflow.
- Primary tap targets are at least 44×44 CSS px.
- Form controls use 16px font on mobile.
- Keyboard focus is visible.
- Reduced motion preserves full comprehension.
