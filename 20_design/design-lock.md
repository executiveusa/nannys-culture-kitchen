# Design Lock — Nanny's Culture Kitchen v1

## Chosen territory
**Living Night Garden** — expressive but controlled.

## Governing idea
A living kitchen where cooking and growing share the same frame.

## Emotional tone
Warm night hospitality, tropical density, brass and fire, curiosity, family, and a small amount of Mardi Gras electricity.

## Signature behavior
The supplied dome hero appears alive through restrained ambient light, steam, and firefly movement. Motion must never delay reading or navigation.

## Why this direction
The supplied image is already ownable. Replacing it with generic restaurant photography would remove the strongest brand asset. The split composition keeps the portrait image intact on desktop while giving the primary worksite offer its own readable surface.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces, because it carries warmth and irregular human character without becoming a generic "luxury serif."
- Utility/body: Inter, already present in the repository and optimized for interface clarity.

## Color behavior
- Night blue: environment / hero / footer.
- Canopy green: plant and food-world structural color.
- Brass: material warmth, title accents, primary action.
- Purple and green Mardi Gras cues: ambient accents only.
- Ember: small operational accent, not a gradient identity.

## Image direction
- Hero source is protected.
- No regeneration or distortion for motion.
- Crop preserves wordmark, dome geometry, growing towers, and kitchen foreground.
- Future photography: food closeups, hands, worksite service, ingredient growing, night atmosphere. No stock wellness smiles.

## Shape language
Rectilinear and architectural. Avoid pill saturation and over-rounded card systems. Thin rules and open editorial spacing separate information.

## Motion philosophy
Motion explains "alive" rather than "interactive tech."
- ambient light pulse
- subtle steam drift
- fireflies
- optional HyperFrames video replacement when a verified asset exists
- native scroll only
- full reduced-motion fallback

## Hero composition
Desktop: readable offer left, protected portrait hero right.
Mobile: hero image first, offer immediately below. No tiny overlay text across the photograph.

## Primary CTA
**Book a worksite lunch**

## Secondary CTA
**See the sample menu**

## Anti-references
- generic vegan cafe
- generic SaaS landing page
- beige wellness editorial
- carnival souvenir shop
- dark "luxury" site with unreadable gold script
- DoorDash clone

## Build authority
Implementation agents may not substitute another palette, font pairing, hero composition, animation system, or CTA without a decision-ledger entry and owner/architect approval.
