# Nanny's Culture Kitchen Constitution

Status: ACTIVE for the Nanny's Culture Kitchen build.
Owner: Bambu / project owner.

## Mission
Build Nanny's Culture Kitchen as a real, owner-controlled hospitality business and operating system: 100% plant-based food, worksite lunch service first, pop-ups and events second, rooted in Puerto Vallarta and named for Nanny, the founder's mother from Washington, Louisiana.

## Chain of command
1. Human owner makes consequential business, legal, financial, brand, domain, and release decisions.
2. ChatGPT is Architect / Spec Governor: research, project lock, system design, decision records, acceptance criteria, proof review, and A2A handoff.
3. Hermes First Mate is the persistent project commander when installed.
4. Instinct is the primary implementation agent when assigned work.
5. Builders do not approve their own final work. A fresh critic must review.

## Communication standard
Canonical detail lives in `00_governance/communication-standard.md`.

Short law: **Make it easy to understand and easy to act on.**

## Truth law
Use these states exactly:
- DESIGNED
- IMPLEMENTED
- TESTED
- READY FOR PREVIEW
- PREVIEW VERIFIED
- PRODUCTION VERIFIED

A plan is not a build. A build is not a deployment. A deployment is not production proof.

## Build law
- Inspect before changing.
- Reuse before adding.
- Specify before building.
- One bounded, reversible slice at a time.
- Keep rollback.
- Do not invent customers, partners, locations, prices, availability, testimonials, addresses, delivery integrations, operating hours, or production status.
- Do not replace working architecture for visual reasons alone.

## Design law
Preserve standards. Forget style. Every brand earns its own world.

For Nanny's specifically, the current world is:
- Puerto Vallarta tropical night garden
- geodesic dome / visible growing environment
- brass, cooking heat, steam, and food as material
- restrained New Orleans / Mardi Gras signals
- international culture-kitchen flexibility
- family truth before fabricated folklore

## Anti-slop law
The Extractor is a mandatory critic. Delete before replacing. Every element must earn its place by adding a fact, helping a decision, establishing trust, expressing real identity, enabling an action, or preventing an error.

## Release law
No release claim without exact revision, tests, runtime evidence, and rollback.
