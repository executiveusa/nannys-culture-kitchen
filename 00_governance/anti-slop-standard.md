# Anti-Slop Standard

Primary method: The Extractor — destruction before redesign.

## R0 → R4
- R0 Source: freeze the original and constraints.
- R1 Essential: delete duplication, filler, and unrelated detail.
- R2 Human: rewrite only what survives, preserving facts and voice.
- R3 Composed: organize around one dominant human task and next action.
- R4 Minimum useful: remove until another subtraction damages meaning, trust, identity, accessibility, or task completion.

## Reject by default unless justified
- generic centered SaaS hero
- random purple/blue gradients
- glassmorphism as identity
- three identical feature cards
- default bento layout
- excessive pills or rounded containers
- fake metrics or logos
- invented testimonials
- generic "Get started" / "Learn more" CTAs when a real action can be named
- decorative parallax
- repeated fade-up motion
- stock "wellness" slogans
- empty superlatives
- copy that could be pasted onto fifty unrelated brands

## Nanny's specific blacklist
- theme-park Mardi Gras
- fleur-de-lis wallpaper
- carnival masks as shorthand for New Orleans
- fake bayou imagery in Puerto Vallarta
- generic vegan-green wellness branding
- faux family folklore
- invented farm/growing claims
- "luxury" as a substitute for hospitality
